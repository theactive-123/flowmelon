01

The challenge

ThermoLab sold premium red-light therapy products on a slow, theme-heavy store. Product pages were cluttered, mobile speed was poor and shoppers dropped off before they ever saw the offer. Every new campaign needed a developer.

02

Our approach

We redesigned the storefront around how people actually shop: clear benefits first, proof early and one obvious path to checkout. Every section was designed in Figma at three breakpoints and reviewed with the team before a line of code.

03

The build

The store was rebuilt with lightweight Liquid sections and a clean component system, with Webflow powering the editorial and landing pages. Images were re-encoded, third-party apps cut by half and the team can now launch products and campaigns on their own.

04

The result

Sales rose 89%, mobile load time dropped by more than half and the marketing team now ships new landing pages in days instead of weeks.
